Compact Mechanical Assemblies

A set of smaller SolidWorks assemblies presented in a compact format. Each model focuses on clean assembly structure, fastener strategy, and clear internal representation using section or exploded views.

SolidWorks Assembly Section View
Plummer Block Bearing Housing
Assembly modelling + internal section representation

Demonstrates a split housing layout and bearing seat alignment. The section view communicates the internal geometry, load path through the base, and the cap-to-body interface used to secure the bearing in position.

SolidWorks Assembly Exploded View
Wheel Support Assembly
Bracket layout + wheel mounting and fastener strategy

Highlights functional grouping of parts (base, side brackets, wheel, pin/axle and fasteners). The exploded view is used to validate assembly order, access for tightening, and alignment of the wheel within the support frame.

SolidWorks Assembly Exploded View
Crane Hook Assembly
Load path clarity + component layout

Presents a compact lifting mechanism with a clear structural hierarchy. The exploded layout communicates how the pulley interfaces with the bracket and how the hook assembly is secured, supporting a clean understanding of assembly sequence and load transfer.
