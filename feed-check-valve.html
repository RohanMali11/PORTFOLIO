Feed Check Valve Assembly

A feed check valve is a control component used in piping systems to allow flow in one direction and prevent reverse flow. In real applications, it protects pumps and boilers from backflow and helps maintain stable pressure in feed lines by ensuring fluid moves only in the intended direction.

Inside the valve, a disc and seat form the sealing interface. When upstream pressure rises, the disc lifts to open the flow path. If pressure drops or flow tries to reverse, the disc returns onto the seat to shut the passage. This simple “open with pressure / close with backflow” behaviour makes check valves essential for safe, reliable operation in industrial water and steam systems.

This SolidWorks assembly focuses on accurate component structure and clear mechanical communication: flange interfaces, internal disc movement, and the relationship between the actuator/handwheel and the valve internals. The section and exploded views are used to show how the valve is assembled and how the internal parts support controlled flow and sealing.

Industrial valve assembly modelled and visualised in SolidWorks with section, exploded and motion study views.

This project demonstrates assembly organisation, mate strategy and motion setup to represent valve opening and closing behaviour. The outputs focus on clear presentation: a hero render, an exploded structure for assembly logic, a section view for internal understanding, and a motion study for functional validation.

Assembly Views & Internal Representation

Figure 1 – Exploded assembly structure. This exploded view communicates the assembly order and how key parts locate: the main body, flange interface, bonnet region, stem/actuation components, and fasteners. It allows a quick check of part relationships, alignment and serviceability.

Figure 2 – Section view (internal mechanism). The section cut reveals the disc/seat interface and the internal flow path through the valve body. It highlights the sealing surfaces and the travel direction of the disc during opening and closing, which is critical to understanding real check-valve behaviour.

Motion Study – Disc Open / Close

The motion study drives the internal disc through its operating travel to visualise opening and closing. This animation is used to validate movement limits, check for interference inside the body, and clearly show how the valve prevents reverse flow in a real piping system.
